# Code Of Conduct

This project expects direct, technical, respectful collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be precise and factual.
- Assume good intent, but verify claims with evidence.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private data or credentials.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing secrets, private chat IDs, personal data, or doxxing material.
- Encouraging fraud, deception, or evasion of legal obligations.
- Spam or off-topic promotion.

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or access when
behavior harms the project or its users.
